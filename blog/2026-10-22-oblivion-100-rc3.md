`1.0.0-rc.3` is out. Most of it is fixes found in `rc.2`: rebirth no longer stacks bonuses or drifts your stats, pickpocketing works, barter handles books and large stacks, and other players stay seated.

Server owners also get two ways to make their server look like their own in the Launcher: an icon in the server list and a landing page in the Join panel.

<!-- truncate -->

## Server branding

Both are optional, and both live in a `branding/` folder next to `config.json`.

* **Server icon.** One image named `icon.*`, shown next to your server's name in the Launcher's server list. It must be PNG, JPEG or WebP, square, from 64x64 to 512x512 pixels and at most 256 KiB. It only shows while the server is listed publicly.
* **Server card.** Your own landing page in the server's Join panel, from `branding/card/index.html`. It is plain HTML and CSS, with no scripts.

See [Branding](/oblivion-mp/docs/Server/branding).

## New

* **Player identity.** A character's name, race, class, sex and birthsign are now saved and synced to other players.
* **Death screen.** A "You died" screen now shows the key to rebirth.
* **Cell respawns.** Server mods can change the cell respawn interval, 72 game hours by default, and force a cell to respawn right away.

## Fixes

* **Sitting.** Other players' characters stood up right after sitting down on chairs and benches.
* **Bonuses on rebirth.** Birthsign and racial bonuses, for example the Warrior's Strength and Endurance or the Redguard's disease and poison resistance, were added again on every rebirth.
* **Attributes on rebirth.** Attributes affected by spells or abilities, such as Speed, drifted further on every rebirth.
* **Stats after death.** A character's stats were overwritten with zeroes after death.
* **Shops.** The game could crash when another player left a shop while you were still inside.
* **Pickpocketing.** Pickpocketing NPCs did nothing.
* **Sneaking.** Being noticed by an NPC simulated by another player stopped you from pickpocketing or talking while sneaking.
* **Books.** Books could not be bought from or sold to merchants.
* **Barter window.** An item bought by another player stayed in your open barter window.
* **Large stacks.** Buying a large stack, such as iron arrows, froze the barter list, made it impossible to scroll and looped the item selection sound.
* **Merchant stock.** Merchants kept their full stock of arrows and similar items after you bought them.
* **Harvesting.** Harvesting plants could randomly skip the game's own activation handling.
* **AI packages.** Some AI packages created for NPCs could be missing their location or target.
* **Cancelled AI orders.** NPCs kept carrying out an AI order after the server had cancelled it.
* **Spawns and loot.** Release servers had no creature spawns or container loot unless a mod set a level scaling policy. The session now defaults to the mid level.

## Updating

* **Servers.** Downloads for both platforms are on the [Installation](/oblivion-mp/docs/Server/installation) page.
* **Mods.** Bump `ReadyM.SDK.Oblivion.Common`, `.Client` and `.Server` to `1.0.0-rc.3`.

Check [Known issues](/oblivion-mp/docs/known-issues) before reporting a bug. Anything not on it, report on the [OblivionMP Discord](https://discord.gg/obmp).
