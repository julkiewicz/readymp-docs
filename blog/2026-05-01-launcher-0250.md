Launcher `0.25.0` brings **PvP matchmaking** to WukongMP. Pick your region, join the queue, and the Launcher pairs you with another player. You no longer have to set up matches yourself or look for opponents on Discord.

<!-- truncate -->

## PvP matchmaking

Open the Launcher, select **PvP**, then **Find Match**. Pick your region and you are in the queue. When a player on the other side is ready, you are both put in the same match: a quick 3-round tournament on a random map.

A few details:

* **Close the game first.** Find Match is disabled while the game is running.
* **The queue shows how long you have been searching.** If nobody turns up, the Launcher tells you, and you can try again.
* **Matches wait for you.** If you already have a match waiting on the server, for example after closing the Launcher, it asks whether you want to join it.

See it in action in the [WukongMP matchmaking video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8rBtOufKPRo).

## Updating

The Launcher updates itself. If you are not on `0.25.0` yet, restart it, or download it from the [Player Portal](https://portal.ready.mp).
