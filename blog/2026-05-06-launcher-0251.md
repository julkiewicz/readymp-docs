Launcher `0.25.1` shows the option to connect to self-hosted WukongMP servers to everyone. Until now it was hidden for most players. It ships alongside [WukongMP SDK 0.2.1](/releases/wukongmp-sdk-021).

<!-- truncate -->

## Changes

* **Direct connect for everyone.** Every account can now connect to a self-hosted server by its address. See the [installation guide](/wukong-mp/docs/Server/installation) to run your own.
* **Bans and whitelists are explained.** If you try to join a server where you are banned or not on the whitelist, the Launcher now tells you: "You're banned from this server" or "You're not whitelisted on this server". See the [admin panel](/wukong-mp/docs/Server/admin-panel) for managing bans and the whitelist on your own server.

## Updating

The Launcher updates itself. If you are not on `0.25.1` yet, restart it, or download it from the [Player Portal](https://portal.ready.mp).
