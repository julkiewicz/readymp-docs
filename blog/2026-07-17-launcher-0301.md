Launcher `0.30.1` came out together with a new OblivionMP alpha build. You need it to connect to the alpha test server, so update the Launcher before you join.

<!-- truncate -->

## Launcher changes

* **.pak files for Oblivion.** Like WukongMP mods since [0.27.0](/releases/readym-launcher-0270), OblivionMP mods can now ship `.pak` files, and the Launcher copies them into the game.
* **Installation fixes.** Several installation steps were fixed for OblivionMP.

## Shipped alongside: the OblivionMP alpha build

The OblivionMP build that goes with this Launcher, based on reports from our testers:

* Added a text chatbox.
* Added UI showing ping and the player count in the current area.
* Nameplates show player names rather than NPC names.
* The pickpocket menu closes automatically when the other player moves away.
* Removed idle NPC dialogue, and the option to start a dialogue with other players.
* Fixed a crash when a pickpocketed player leaves the area.
* Fixed the Clairvoyance spell to work for each player on their own.
* Fixed equipped enchanted items showing their effects when a player spawns or enters the area.

## Updating

The Launcher updates itself. If you are not on `0.30.1` yet, restart it, or download it from the [Player Portal](https://portal.ready.mp).
